The register

6 events pass the inclusion rule and cannot be tied to a place with a source. They are not deleted and they are never pinned. Each one is published here with the reason it cannot be located and the evidence that would unlock it.

Publishing what cannot be proven is part of the method. A map that quietly drops the events it cannot place looks more complete than it is, and the gaps it hides are exactly where the archives are closed. Every entry below is an open research question.

If you can supply the missing evidence, the correction channel is corrections@stillonrecord.org. Include the record id and the source.

Entries

Persian famine

1917–1919, Northern and western Persia

Why it cannot be placed: Only city-level data compiled is Afkhami's influenza table; famine, cholera, typhus and flu deaths were never separated by place.

What would place it: Provincial grain requisition records from British, Russian and Ottoman military archives.

Leaded petrol exposure

1923–2021, Global, wherever leaded petrol was sold

Why it cannot be placed: Exposure followed a distributed fuel across most of the world for 98 years.

What would place it: Nothing would; national blood-lead series are the correct unit, and source sites are pinned separately.

Tobacco industry concealment

1953–2006, United States and globally

Why it cannot be placed: The harm is a corporate course of conduct with no geography.

What would place it: Nothing would; this belongs in a register permanently.

Kafala system deaths

1970–2025, Gulf states

Why it cannot be placed: Deaths recorded by nationality and employer sector, never by workplace.

What would place it: Employer-level mortality reporting; does not currently exist in any Gulf state.

Expulsion of the Hoa and the boat people

1978–1990, Ho Chi Minh City; Haiphong; South China Sea routes

Why it cannot be placed: The largest share of deaths occurred at sea and is inherently unlocatable.

What would place it: Nothing would; departure ports can be pinned, deaths cannot.

Qatar World Cup construction deaths

2010–2022, Doha

Why it cannot be placed: Circulating totals are national migrant-death counts, not site-attributed.

What would place it: Site-level fatality reporting by contractor; never published.
