Still on Record

A sourced map of deliberately caused mass suffering, 1500 to now.

About

Still on Record is an index of events where people were harmed deliberately, or where the harm was a foreseen cost that someone accepted. It covers genocide and state killing, colonial conquest, slavery and forced labour, famine used as a weapon, forced displacement, religious persecution, institutional abuse, and industrial harm where the producer knew.

What it is not

Not a ranking. There is no total, no leaderboard and no score. The categories are not comparable and the death figures are not summable. Not a verdict either: where proceedings are live, the record names the case and stops there.

Status

Seed stage. This build carries 62 records from the development fixture, with coordinates that are approximate and unverified. The full dataset holds 541 records, each naming a real and relevant source, and none of those sources has yet been opened and checked one by one. Records carry their state on their own page. Read it before citing anything here.

The name

These events are still on record. The documents survive, the judgments were handed down, the registers were kept. Denial works by betting that nobody will go and look. This project goes and looks, and shows its working.

Who runs it

A stichting registered in the Netherlands. Board, policy plan and annual figures are published. No advertising, no paywall, no sponsored content. Hosting is covered by donations; research and development work is funded by grants and commissioned projects, and the accounts are public.
