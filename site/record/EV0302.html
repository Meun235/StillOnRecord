Tobacco industry concealment

1953–2006, United States and globally, Multiple

Corporate and industrial harm. Tier 1, confirmed.

Seeded, not yet verified. The source below is real and relevant, and nobody has yet opened it to confirm it says what this record claims. Treat it as a lead until that happens. How verification works

Court found a decades-long scheme to deceive.

On the figures

WHO attributes more than 8 million deaths a year to tobacco, and around 100 million deaths to tobacco across the twentieth century. The litigation established the concealment; the mortality is epidemiological attribution, not a body count.

Why this is not on the map

The harm is a corporate course of conduct with no geography.

What would place it: Nothing would; this belongs in a register permanently.
