Discovery of tetraethyl lead

1921, General Motors Research Laboratory, Dayton, Ohio, United States

Corporate and industrial harm. Tier 1, confirmed.

Seeded, not yet verified. The source below is real and relevant, and nobody has yet opened it to confirm it says what this record claims. Treat it as a lead until that happens. How verification works

Ethanol was a known, unpatentable alternative anti-knock agent. Tetraethyl lead could be patented. Midgley later invented CFCs.
