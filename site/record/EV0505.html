Hawk's Nest Tunnel silicosis disaster

1930–1935, Gauley Bridge, West Virginia, United States

Corporate and industrial harm. Tier 1, confirmed.

Seeded, not yet verified. The source below is real and relevant, and nobody has yet opened it to confirm it says what this record claims. Treat it as a lead until that happens. How verification works

Dry drilling through almost pure silica without respirators, while engineers wore them on inspection visits. Many of the dead were buried in unmarked graves in a nearby field.

On the figures

The lower figure is the 1936 congressional count; the higher is Cherniack's epidemiological reconstruction.
